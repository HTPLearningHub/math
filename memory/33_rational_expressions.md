## Corrections to the source

* Source omits excluded values in most examples; book gives them everywhere, including those
  of cancelled brackets.
* Source Example 4 (division) gives no excluded values; the zeros of the flipped top ($-3, 2$)
  are excluded too (§ 5.3, shown with $0 \div 0$).
* Source LCD = "product of every unique factor" - wrong with repeated factors; book: each factor
  as many times as the most in one bottom (§ 6.2).
* Source "radicals in the denominator are mathematically unsimplified" - book says it is an
  agreement about the finished form; the value is the same.
* Source writes $\cdot$ for multiplication and uses $\cancel$; book uses $\times$ and shows
  cancelling in steps (and fig_01).
