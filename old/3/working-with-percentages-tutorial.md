# Working With Percentages: A Complete Beginner-Friendly Guide

## 1. Introduction
Percentages are one of the most common mathematical tools used in daily life [12]. Whether you are checking a discount at a store ($30\%$ off), leaving a tip at a restaurant ($20\%$ tip), predicting the weather ($50\%$ chance of rain), or checking your grade on a math quiz ($90\%$), percentages help us understand proportions quickly [12].

The word **percent** comes from the Latin phrase *per centum* [12]. In Latin, *centum* means **one hundred** [12]. Therefore, **percent** literally means **"out of a hundred"** or **"per hundred"** [12]. 

A percentage is simply a way of expressing a fraction or ratio as a part out of $100$ [12]. Why do we use $100$?
* **Easy for the human brain:** $100$ is small enough for our minds to easily picture and comprehend [12].
* **Detailed enough:** $100$ is large enough to provide enough fine steps (gradations) between $0$ and $100$ to make meaningful comparisons [12].

---

## 2. Prerequisite Knowledge
To understand percentages thoroughly, you only need to know a few basic ideas about numbers:

1. **Fractions:** A fraction represents a part of a whole quantity, written as $\frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}}$.
   * The **numerator** (top number) counts how many parts you have.
   * The **denominator** (bottom number) tells you how many equal parts make up the complete whole.
2. **Ratios:** A ratio compares two numbers to show their relative size (for example, $9 : 10$ or $\frac{9}{10}$).
3. **Simplifying Fractions:** Reducing a fraction to its lowest terms by dividing both top and bottom numbers by their **Greatest Common Factor (GCF)**.
   * Example: $\frac{80}{100} = \frac{80 \div 20}{100 \div 20} = \frac{4}{5}$ [12].
4. **Decimals and Place Value:** Decimals are another way to write fractions based on powers of ten ($10, 100, 1000$).
   * The first digit after the decimal point is the **tenths** place ($\frac{1}{10} = 0.1$).
   * The second digit after the decimal point is the **hundredths** place ($\frac{1}{100} = 0.01$).

---

## 3. Important Definitions

* **Percent ($\%$):** A ratio or fraction that compares a number to $100$ [12].
* **Ratio:** A mathematical comparison of two quantities by division [12].
* **Equivalent Ratios:** Two ratios that express the same proportional relationship, created by multiplying or dividing both numbers by the same non-zero amount [12].
* **Greatest Common Factor (GCF):** The largest whole number that divides two or more numbers evenly without leaving a remainder.
* **Simplifying (Reducing) a Fraction:** Rewriting a fraction so that its numerator and denominator share no common factors other than $1$ [12].

## 4. Main Concepts

### Concept 1: Percentages as Standardized Ratios
Suppose you take three different quizzes with different total points [12]:
* Quiz A: You score $9$ points out of $10$ [12].
* Quiz B: You score $18$ points out of $20$ [12].
* Quiz C: You score $450$ points out of $500$ [12].

Which score is the highest? It is difficult to compare $9/10$, $18/20$, and $450/500$ immediately because the totals ($10, 20, 500$) are all different [12]. 

Percentages solve this problem by converting every total into $100$ [12]:
* For $9/10$: Multiply top and bottom by $10$ to get $\frac{9 \times 10}{10 \times 10} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].
* For $18/20$: Multiply top and bottom by $5$ to get $\frac{18 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].
* For $450/500$: Divide top and bottom by $5$ to get $\frac{450 \div 5}{500 \div 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].

All three scores represent the exact same proportion: **$90\%$** [12]!

```
Original Ratio  -->  Scale Factor to Base 100  -->  Percentage
  9 / 10             (Multiply by 10/10)            90 / 100  =  90%
 18 / 20             (Multiply by 5/5)              90 / 100  =  90%
450 / 500            (Divide by 5/5)                90 / 100  =  90%
```

### Concept 2: Converting Between Percentages, Fractions, and Decimals
Every percentage can be represented in three equivalent forms [12]:

1. **Percent to Fraction:**
   * Step 1: Write the percentage number over $100$: $\frac{P}{100}$ [12].
   * Step 2: Simplify the fraction if possible by dividing by the GCF [12].
   * Example: $80\% = \frac{80}{100} = \frac{4}{5}$ [12].

2. **Percent to Decimal:**
   * Divide the percentage by $100$ (move the decimal point two places to the left) [12].
   * Example: $80\% = \frac{80}{100} = 0.80$ [12].

3. **Decimal to Percent:**
   * Multiply the decimal by $100$ (move the decimal point two places to the right) and add the $\%$ sign.
   * Example: $0.03 \times 100 = 3\%$ [12].

4. **Fraction to Percent:**
   * Convert the fraction to a denominator of $100$, OR divide numerator by denominator to get a decimal, then multiply by $100\%$.
   * Example: $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{50}{100} = 50\%$ [12].

## 5. Formulas and Rules

### 1. Basic Percentage Formula
$$\text{Percentage } (\%) = \left( \frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}} \right) \times 100$$

Where:
* **Part:** The specific portion or quantity you are evaluating [12].
* **Whole:** The total magnitude or baseline amount [12].

### 2. Percentage to Simplified Fraction Rule
$$\text{Fraction} = \frac{P}{100} \xrightarrow{\text{Divide numerator and denominator by GCF}} \frac{P \div \text{GCF}}{100 \div \text{GCF}}$$

### 3. Finding the Part from a Percentage
$$\text{Part} = \left( \frac{P}{100} \right) \times \text{Whole}$$

### 4. Decimal Conversion Rules
$$\text{Decimal} = \frac{P}{100} = P \times 0.01$$
$$\text{Percent} = \text{Decimal} \times 100\%$$

## 6. Step-by-Step Examples

### Easy Level Examples

#### Example 1: Convert $3\%$ into a Fraction and Decimal [12]
* **Problem:** Express $3\%$ as a fraction in simplest form and as a decimal [12].
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Write $3\%$ as a fraction over $100$:
     $$\frac{3}{100}$$
  2. Check if $\frac{3}{100}$ can be simplified. Since $3$ is a prime number and does not divide $100$ evenly, it cannot be simplified further [12].
  3. Convert to decimal by dividing $3$ by $100$:
     $$3 \div 100 = 0.03$$
* **Final Answer:** Fraction = $\frac{3}{100}$, Decimal = $0.03$ [12].

#### Example 2: Convert $50\%$ into a Simplified Fraction [12]
* **Problem:** Simplify $50\%$ into its simplest fraction form [12].
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Write $50\%$ as a fraction over $100$:
     $$\frac{50}{100}$$
  2. Find the GCF of $50$ and $100$, which is $50$ [12].
  3. Divide numerator and denominator by $50$:
     $$\frac{50 \div 50}{100 \div 50} = \frac{1}{2}$$
* **Final Answer:** $\frac{1}{2}$ [12].

---

### Medium Level Examples

#### Example 3: Convert $80\%$ into a Simplified Fraction [12]
* **Problem:** Express $80\%$ as a fully simplified fraction [12].
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Write $80\%$ over $100$:
     $$\frac{80}{100}$$
  2. Identify common factors. Both $80$ and $100$ end in $0$, so they divide by $10$:
     $$\frac{80 \div 10}{100 \div 10} = \frac{8}{10}$$
  3. Both $8$ and $10$ are even, so divide by $2$ (or divide by $20$ directly) [12]:
     $$\frac{8 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{4}{5}$$
* **Final Answer:** $\frac{4}{5}$ [12].

#### Example 4: Calculating a Score Percentage [12]
* **Problem:** You score $18$ points out of a total $20$ points on a quiz. What is your percentage score [12]?
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Identify Part = $18$ and Whole = $20$ [12].
  2. Form the ratio:
     $$\frac{18}{20}$$
  3. Determine the factor needed to scale the denominator $20$ up to $100$:
     $$20 \times 5 = 100$$
  4. Multiply both numerator and denominator by $5$ [12]:
     $$\frac{18 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$$
* **Final Answer:** $90\%$ [12].

---

### Challenging Level Examples

#### Example 5: Finding a Tip Amount
* **Problem:** You have a meal at a restaurant that costs $\$45.00$. You want to leave a $20\%$ tip [12]. How much money is the tip?
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Convert $20\%$ into a fraction or decimal:
     $$20\% = \frac{20}{100} = \frac{1}{5} = 0.20$$
  2. Multiply the total bill by $0.20$ or $\frac{1}{5}$:
     $$\text{Tip} = 45 \times 0.20 = 9.00$$
     $$\text{OR } \text{Tip} = \frac{45}{5} = 9.00$$
* **Final Answer:** The tip is $\$9.00$. Total paid = $\$45.00 + \$9.00 = \$54.00$.

#### Example 6: Discount Calculation [12]
* **Problem:** A pair of shoes costs $\$120.00$. Today there is a $30\%$ discount [12]. What is the sale price of the shoes?
* **Step-by-Step Solution:**
  1. Calculate the discount amount ($30\%$ of $\$120$):
     $$30\% = \frac{30}{100} = 0.30$$
     $$\text{Discount Amount} = 120 \times 0.30 = 36.00$$
  2. Subtract the discount from the original price:
     $$\text{Sale Price} = 120.00 - 36.00 = 84.00$$
* **Final Answer:** The sale price is $\$84.00$.

## 8. Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Confusing Small Percentages with Decimals
* **Incorrect:** Thinking that $3\% = 0.3$
* **Why it's wrong:** $0.3$ is equal to $\frac{3}{10} = \frac{30}{100} = 30\%$. 
* **Correct Approach:** $3\% = \frac{3}{100} = 0.03$ [12]. Always move the decimal point **two** places to the left!

### Mistake 2: Forgetting to Multiply/Divide Both Numerator and Denominator
* **Incorrect:** Converting $\frac{18}{20}$ to percent by calculating $18 \times 5 = 90$ without keeping the denominator equal to $100$.
* **Correct Approach:** Always perform the exact same operation on both top and bottom numbers: $\frac{18 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].

### Mistake 3: Stopping Before the Fraction is Fully Simplified
* **Incorrect:** Writing $80\% = \frac{8}{10}$ and stopping there.
* **Why it's wrong:** Both $8$ and $10$ are still divisible by $2$.
* **Correct Approach:** Fully reduce to lowest terms: $\frac{8 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{4}{5}$ [12].

## 10. Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Write $25\%$ over $100$: $\frac{25}{100}$.
2. Divide numerator and denominator by GCF ($25$): $\frac{25 \div 25}{100 \div 25} = \frac{1}{4}$.
3. Convert to decimal: $25 \div 100 = 0.25$.
* **Answer:** Fraction = $\frac{1}{4}$, Decimal = $0.25$.

### Solution 2
1. Write $7\%$ over $100$: $\frac{7}{100}$.
2. Since $7$ is prime, $\frac{7}{100}$ is already simplified.
3. Convert to decimal: $7 \div 100 = 0.07$.
* **Answer:** Fraction = $\frac{7}{100}$, Decimal = $0.07$.

### Solution 3
1. Set up the fraction: $\frac{16}{20}$.
2. Scale denominator to $100$: $20 \times 5 = 100$.
3. Multiply top and bottom by $5$: $\frac{16 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{80}{100} = 80\%$.
* **Answer:** $80\%$.

### Solution 4
1. Convert $30\%$ to decimal: $30\% = 0.30 = \frac{3}{10}$.
2. Multiply by $\$80$: $80 \times 0.30 = 24.00$.
* **Answer:** $\$24.00$.

### Solution 5
1. Set up fraction: $\frac{3}{5}$.
2. Scale denominator $5$ to $100$: $5 \times 20 = 100$.
3. Multiply top and bottom by $20$: $\frac{3 \times 20}{5 \times 20} = \frac{60}{100} = 60\%$.
* **Answer:** $60\%$.

### Solution 6
1. Calculate discount: $15\% \text{ of } 500 = \frac{15}{100} \times 500 = 15 \times 5 = \$75.00$.
2. Calculate final price: $\$500.00 - \$75.00 = \$425.00$.
* **Answer:** Discount = $\$75.00$, Final Price = $\$425.00$.

### Solution 7
1. Test 1 percentage: $\frac{18 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].
2. Test 2 percentage: $\frac{450 \div 5}{500 \div 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%$ [12].
3. Both scores are exactly equal ($90\%$) [12].
* **Answer:** Test 1 = $90\%$, Test 2 = $90\%$. Neither performance was higher; both represent the exact same proportion [12].

## 11. Summary
A **percentage** is a specific ratio that compares a quantity to $100$ [12]. The symbol $\%$ means "out of one hundred" [12]. Percentages make it easy to compare fractions that have different total bases by standardizing every total into $100$ [12]. You can freely convert between percentages, fractions, and decimals: divide by $100$ to get a fraction/decimal, or multiply by $100$ to get a percentage [12].

---

## 12. Key Things to Remember

* **Percent Definition:** Percent = "Out of 100" ($P\% = \frac{P}{100}$) [12].
* **To convert Percent to Decimal:** Move decimal point **2 places left** (e.g., $80\% \rightarrow 0.80$, $3\% \rightarrow 0.03$) [12].
* **To convert Decimal to Percent:** Move decimal point **2 places right** (e.g., $0.50 \rightarrow 50\%$).
* **To convert Percent to Fraction:** Place over $100$ and divide top and bottom by their GCF [12].
* **Proportional Scaling Rule:** Whatever operation you do to the denominator to reach $100$, do the exact same operation to the numerator [12]!
