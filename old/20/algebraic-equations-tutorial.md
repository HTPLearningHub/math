# Algebraic Equations and Their Solutions

### 1. Introduction

An **algebraic equation** is a mathematical statement showing that two expressions are equal. Solving an equation means finding the exact value of an unknown variable that makes the equation true.

Learning how to solve basic algebraic equations is one of the most fundamental skills in mathematics. It allows us to calculate unknown values in science, business, technology, and everyday life. While simple equations can often be solved mentally using common sense, mastering a systematic, step-by-step approach is essential for solving more complex mathematical problems.

---

### 2. Prerequisite Knowledge

Before solving algebraic equations, you should understand a few basic concepts:

*   **Arithmetic Operations**: You should be comfortable with basic addition ($+$), subtraction ($-$), multiplication ($\times$ or $\cdot$), and division ($\div$ or $/$).
*   **Variables**: A variable is a letter (such as $x$, $n$, $d$, or $y$) used to represent an unknown number.
*   **Order of Operations (PEMDAS)**: The standard sequence used to evaluate expressions:
    1.  **P**arentheses
    2.  **E**xponents
    3.  **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision (from left to right)
    4.  **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction (from left to right)

---

### 3. Important Definitions

*   **Algebraic Equation**: A mathematical sentence containing an equal sign ($=$) with numbers, variables, and operations on both sides (for example, $x + 2 = 5$).
*   **Variable**: A symbol, usually a letter, that stands for an unknown quantity.
*   **Inverse Operation**: The mathematical operation that "undoes" or reverses another operation. 
    *   Addition and Subtraction are inverse operations.
    *   Multiplication and Division are inverse operations.
*   **Isolating the Variable**: The process of performing algebraic steps to get the variable all by itself on one side of the equal sign (for example, $x = 3$).

---

### 4. Main Concepts

#### The Golden Rule of Algebra
To maintain equality, **whatever operation you perform on one side of an equation, you MUST perform on the exact other side**. Think of an equation like a balanced scale: if you add or remove weight from one side, you must do the exact same thing to the other side to keep it balanced.

#### Inverse Operations
To isolate a variable, look at what operation is being applied to it, and systematically undo that operation using its **inverse**:
*   To undo **addition**, **subtract** the same amount from both sides.
*   To undo **subtraction**, **add** the same amount to both sides.
*   To undo **multiplication**, **divide** both sides by the same amount.
*   To undo **division**, **multiply** both sides by the same amount.

#### Reverse PEMDAS Order for Solving Equations
When evaluating an expression, we follow the order of **PEMDAS**. However, when **solving** an equation (undoing operations to isolate a variable), we work in **reverse PEMDAS order**:
1. First, undo **Addition and Subtraction**.
2. Next, undo **Multiplication and Division**.
3. Then, undo **Exponents and Parentheses**.

---

### 5. Formulas and Rules

#### Properties of Equality
If $A = B$, then for any real number $c$:

1.  **Addition Property of Equality**:
    $$A + c = B + c$$
2.  **Subtraction Property of Equality**:
    $$A - c = B - c$$
3.  **Multiplication Property of Equality**:
    $$A \cdot c = B \cdot c$$
4.  **Division Property of Equality** (where $c \neq 0$):
    $$\frac{A}{c} = \frac{B}{c}$$

#### Rule for Negative Variables
If isolating a variable leaves a negative sign in front of the variable (e.g., $-b = c$), multiply or divide both sides by $-1$ to isolate the positive variable:
$$\text{If } -b = c, \text{ then } b = -c$$

### 6. Step-by-Step Examples

#### Example 1 (Easy - Addition)
**Problem:** Solve for $x$:
$$x + 2 = 5$$

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**
1.  **Identify the operation:** The variable $x$ has $2$ added to it.
2.  **Apply the inverse operation:** The inverse of addition is subtraction. Subtract $2$ from both sides of the equation.
    $$(x + 2) - 2 = 5 - 2$$
3.  **Simplify:** On the left, $+2 - 2 = 0$, leaving $x$ alone. On the right, $5 - 2 = 3$.
    $$x = 3$$

---

#### Example 2 (Easy - Subtraction)
**Problem:** Solve for $n$:
$$n - 4 = 17$$

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**
1.  **Identify the operation:** The variable $n$ has $4$ subtracted from it.
2.  **Apply the inverse operation:** The inverse of subtraction is addition. Add $4$ to both sides.
    $$(n - 4) + 4 = 17 + 4$$
3.  **Simplify:** On the left, $-4 + 4 = 0$, leaving $n$. On the right, $17 + 4 = 21$.
    $$n = 21$$

---

#### Example 3 (Easy - Multiplication)
**Problem:** Solve for $d$:
$$4d = 20$$

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**
1.  **Identify the operation:** The variable $d$ is multiplied by $4$.
2.  **Apply the inverse operation:** The inverse of multiplication is division. Divide both sides by $4$.
    $$\frac{4d}{4} = \frac{20}{4}$$
3.  **Simplify:** On the left, $\frac{4}{4} = 1$, leaving $1d$ or simply $d$. On the right, $\frac{20}{4} = 5$.
    $$d = 5$$

---

#### Example 4 (Medium - Division)
**Problem:** Solve for $y$:
$$\frac{y}{8} = 3$$

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**
1.  **Identify the operation:** The variable $y$ is divided by $8$.
2.  **Apply the inverse operation:** The inverse of division is multiplication. Multiply both sides by $8$.
    $$\left(\frac{y}{8}\right) \cdot 8 = 3 \cdot 8$$
3.  **Simplify:** On the left, dividing by $8$ and multiplying by $8$ cancel out, leaving $y$. On the right, $3 \cdot 8 = 24$.
    $$y = 24$$

---

#### Example 5 (Challenging - Multi-Step with Negative Variable)
**Problem:** Solve for $b$:
$$4 - b = 7$$

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**
1.  **Identify the operations:** The number $4$ is added to $-b$ (since $4 - b$ is equivalent to $-b + 4$). Following reverse PEMDAS, handle addition/subtraction first.
2.  **Step 1 — Undo the positive 4:** Subtract $4$ from both sides.
    $$(4 - b) - 4 = 7 - 4$$
    $$-b = 3$$
3.  **Step 2 — Undo the negative sign:** We need positive $b$, not $-b$. Divide or multiply both sides by $-1$.
    $$\frac{-b}{-1} = \frac{3}{-1}$$
    $$b = -3$$

### 7. Visual Explanations

#### Summary of Inverse Operations

| Given Operation on Variable | Inverse Operation Needed | Action on Both Sides | Example Equation | Solution Step | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Addition ($+a$)** | Subtraction ($-a$) | Subtract $a$ | $x + 2 = 5$ | $x = 5 - 2$ | $x = 3$ |
| **Subtraction ($-a$)** | Addition ($+a$) | Add $a$ | $n - 4 = 17$ | $n = 17 + 4$ | $n = 21$ |
| **Multiplication ($\cdot a$)**| Division ($\div a$) | Divide by $a$ | $4d = 20$ | $d = \frac{20}{4}$ | $d = 5$ |
| **Division ($\div a$)** | Multiplication ($\cdot a$)| Multiply by $a$ | $\frac{y}{8} = 3$ | $y = 3 \cdot 8$ | $y = 24$ |

#### The Reverse PEMDAS Workflow Diagram

```text
[ Evaluating Expressions ]                [ Solving Equations ]
   Follow Forward PEMDAS                    Follow Reverse PEMDAS
----------------------------             ----------------------------
  1. Parentheses                            1. Addition & Subtraction
  2. Exponents                              2. Multiplication & Division
  3. Multiplication & Division              3. Exponents
  4. Addition & Subtraction                 4. Parentheses
```

### 8. Common Mistakes

1.  **Doing an operation to only one side of the equation**
    *   *Incorrect:* For $x + 2 = 5$, subtracting $2$ from the left side only to get $x = 5$.
    *   *Correct:* Subtract $2$ from **both** sides: $x = 5 - 2 = 3$.

2.  **Using the same operation instead of the inverse operation**
    *   *Incorrect:* For $n - 4 = 17$, subtracting $4$ again: $n = 17 - 4 = 13$.
    *   *Correct:* Use the **inverse** of subtraction, which is addition: $n = 17 + 4 = 21$.

3.  **Forgetting to handle the negative sign of a variable**
    *   *Incorrect:* Stopping at $-b = 3$ and claiming the answer is $3$.
    *   *Correct:* Divide both sides by $-1$ to isolate positive $b$: $b = -3$.

4.  **Undoing operations in the wrong order**
    *   *Incorrect:* Trying to divide by coefficient before dealing with added/subtracted constants without applying division to all terms.
    *   *Correct:* Always undo Addition/Subtraction **before** Multiplication/Division (Reverse PEMDAS).

### 9. Practice Problems

Solve the following equations for the given variable. The problems increase in difficulty from Easy to Challenging.

1.  **Problem 1 (Easy):** Solve for $a$:
    $$a + 7 = 12$$
2.  **Problem 2 (Easy):** Solve for $k$:
    $$k - 9 = 5$$
3.  **Problem 3 (Medium):** Solve for $m$:
    $$6m = 42$$
4.  **Problem 4 (Medium):** Solve for $p$:
    $$\frac{p}{5} = 6$$
5.  **Problem 5 (Challenging):** Solve for $w$:
    $$10 - w = 15$$

---

### 10. Solutions

#### Solution to Problem 1
$$a + 7 = 12$$
*   **Step 1:** Subtract $7$ from both sides to undo addition.
    $$a + 7 - 7 = 12 - 7$$
*   **Step 2:** Simplify.
    $$a = 5$$

#### Solution to Problem 2
$$k - 9 = 5$$
*   **Step 1:** Add $9$ to both sides to undo subtraction.
    $$k - 9 + 9 = 5 + 9$$
*   **Step 2:** Simplify.
    $$k = 14$$

#### Solution to Problem 3
$$6m = 42$$
*   **Step 1:** Divide both sides by $6$ to undo multiplication.
    $$\frac{6m}{6} = \frac{42}{6}$$
*   **Step 2:** Simplify.
    $$m = 7$$

#### Solution to Problem 4
$$\frac{p}{5} = 6$$
*   **Step 1:** Multiply both sides by $5$ to undo division.
    $$\left(\frac{p}{5}\right) \cdot 5 = 6 \cdot 5$$
*   **Step 2:** Simplify.
    $$p = 30$$

#### Solution to Solution to Problem 5
$$10 - w = 15$$
*   **Step 1:** Subtract $10$ from both sides.
    $$(10 - w) - 10 = 15 - 10$$
    $$-w = 5$$
*   **Step 2:** Divide both sides by $-1$ to solve for positive $w$.
    $$\frac{-w}{-1} = \frac{5}{-1}$$
    $$w = -5$$

---

### 11. Summary

Solving an algebraic equation requires isolating the variable by applying inverse operations step-by-step. To maintain balance, whatever operation is performed on one side must also be performed on the other side. When multiple operations are present, work in **reverse PEMDAS order** (undoing addition and subtraction first, followed by multiplication and division).

---

### 12. Key Things to Remember

*   **Golden Rule:** Keep the equation balanced by performing the exact same action on both sides.
*   **Inverse Pairs:**
    *   Addition $\leftrightarrow$ Subtraction
    *   Multiplication $\leftrightarrow$ Division
*   **Order of Operations:** Undo operations in **reverse PEMDAS order**.
*   **Negative Variables:** If you end up with $-x = c$, multiply or divide by $-1$ to get $x = -c$.
*   **Verification:** Always check your solution by plugging your answer back into the original equation!
